Hey everyone!

Today, we're announcing and releasing SolyncORBIT, our brand new account system made for everything Solync. You can [click here to try it out](https://orbit.solync.org).

SolyncORBIT was made to answer a big question, how do we nail the user experience of a passwordless authentication system? Our answer is to give you a one time password, sent to your email, ready to use. You also have other options to log into SolyncORBIT without a password, such as Google, UNTONE, or Discord.

If a passwordless account system isn't your thing, we're also giving you a way to enter an optional password for your account.

We also want to mention that past Potaro.Wiki and Camellia.Wiki accounts will be migrated to SolyncORBIT, just log in with your original Discord account and SolyncORBIT is ready to use!
